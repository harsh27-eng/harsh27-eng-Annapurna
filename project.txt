PROJECT ANNAPURNA
SURPLUS FOOD REDISTRIBUTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Version: 1.0

Objective:
Connect food donors with NGOs so that surplus food can be
redistributed instead of being wasted.

Features:
1. Donor Registration/Login
2. NGO Registration/Login
3. Add Surplus Food
4. View Available Donations
5. NGO Claims Donation

Main Users:
- Food Donor
- NGO
