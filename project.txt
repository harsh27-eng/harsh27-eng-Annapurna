PROJECT ANNAPURNA
SURPLUS FOOD REDISTRIBUTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Version: 2.0

Objective:
Provide a real-time platform connecting food donors,
NGOs and volunteers for safe redistribution of surplus food.

Features:

1. Donor Registration/Login
2. NGO Registration/Login
3. Add Surplus Food
4. View Available Donations
5. NGO Claims Donation
6. Automated Food Expiry Tracking
7. Automatic Cancellation of Expired Food
8. Geo-Location Based NGO Matching
9. Real-Time Notifications
10. Volunteer/Driver Assignment
11. Delivery Tracking

Users:
- Food Donor
- NGO
- Volunteer/Driver

Technical Features:
- Geolocation matching
- Automated expiry tracking
- Role-based dashboards
- SMS notifications
- Delivery tracking
