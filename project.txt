PROJECT ANNAPURNA
SURPLUS FOOD REDISTRIBUTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Version: 1.1

Objective:
Connect food donors with NGOs so that surplus food can be
redistributed safely and efficiently.

Features:
1. Donor Registration/Login
2. NGO Registration/Login
3. Add Surplus Food
4. View Available Donations
5. NGO Claims Donation
6. Automated Food Expiry Tracking
7. Expired Donations are Automatically Cancelled

Food Safety:
- Store the time when food was cooked
- Calculate the safe consumption period
- Monitor food expiry
- Cancel donations that exceed the safe consumption window

Main Users:
- Food Donor
- NGO
