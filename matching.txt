PROJECT ANNAPURNA
SMART MATCHING MODULE

Version: 2.0

Geo-Location Matching:

1. Obtain the donor's location.
2. Obtain locations of registered NGOs.
3. Calculate the distance between the donor and NGOs.
4. Identify nearby NGOs.
5. Notify suitable NGOs about the available food.
6. Allow an NGO to claim the donation.

Purpose:
To reduce the time required to find a suitable NGO
for collecting surplus food.
